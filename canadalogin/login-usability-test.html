ICAM · Test desktop

Open this on a computer

The ICAM test desktop simulates a full computer screen with a browser, mail and text notifications. It needs a window at least 760 pixels wide.

This is a testing environment. Nothing here is real: no real accounts, emails, texts or calls are used.

Ceci est un environnement de test. Rien n’est réel.

Welcome to the test session

Thank you for taking part. In a moment you’ll see something that looks like a computer desktop, with a web browser, email, text messages and phone calls.

This is a testing environment. Nothing here is real. No real accounts, emails, texts or calls are used, and you can’t break anything.

Your facilitator will guide you over the video call. Please share your screen when they ask, then select Start.

Turn your sound on: some steps may include a phone call.
